Privacy Policy

Asawir Gold Calculator
Last updated: October 07, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Asawir Gold Calculator ("the App") handles information when you use the App.

1. Information We Collect

Asawir Gold Calculator does not require you to create an account or provide personal information to use the App.

We do not intentionally collect or store your name, email address, phone number, physical address, contacts, photos, camera data, microphone data, location data, or social media information.

2. Calculator Information

You may enter information such as gold weight, gold purity, GST, hallmark charges, packing charges, and other values required for calculating an estimated jewellery price.

These values are used to perform the calculation and provide the requested estimate. The App does not require an account to save this information.

3. Live Gold Price

The App connects to our backend service to retrieve the current/live gold price required by the calculator.

The backend service is used only to provide live gold-price information. It does not collect or store personal information from users through the App.

4. Analytics, Advertising and Tracking

Asawir Gold Calculator does not use advertising services, analytics services, or tracking technologies to create user profiles or track users across apps or websites.

5. Permissions

The App does not require access to your camera, photos, microphone, contacts, precise location, or other sensitive device permissions for its core functionality.

6. Third-Party Services

The App communicates with our backend service solely to retrieve live gold price information. The App does not use third-party services to collect personal information for analytics, advertising, or user tracking.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Because the App does not require an account and does not collect or store personal information, there is no user account or personal profile for us to retain or delete.

8. Children's Privacy

The App is a general-purpose gold and jewellery price calculation utility. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the App and the services used to provide live gold-price information. However, no method of transmission over the Internet can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page, together with an updated "Last updated" date.
